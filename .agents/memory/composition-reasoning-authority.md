name: Composition reasoning authority
description: Versioning, precedence, and provenance rules for authoritative composition planning.
---

Composition Intelligence v2 decisions follow song intent → dramatic arc → section function → phrase intent → instrument role → motif → harmony/rhythm/voicing → event precedence. Historical plans without this contract remain v1 and must not silently acquire v2 behavior. Musical constraint, resolution, and voice-leading claims are valid only when recomputed from final performed events after modulation, filtering, and humanization.

**Why:** Musical plans are only authoritative if repeated material and performed events derive from the same ordered decisions, while deterministic regeneration requires the exact seed and accepted evidence identity to survive every downstream engine.

**How to apply:** Any new composition policy must fit into the declared precedence, remain inert for v1 plans, propagate composition version plus evidence identity into final TrackModel provenance, and mark final-event metrics unavailable rather than scoring partial voices or missing resolutions as success.
