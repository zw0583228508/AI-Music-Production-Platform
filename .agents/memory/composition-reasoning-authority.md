description: Versioning, precedence, and provenance rules for authoritative composition planning.
---

Composition Intelligence v2 decisions follow song intent → dramatic arc → section function → phrase intent → instrument role → motif → harmony/rhythm/voicing → event precedence. Historical plans without this contract remain v1 and must not silently acquire v2 behavior.

**Why:** Musical plans are only authoritative if repeated material and performed events derive from the same ordered decisions, while deterministic regeneration requires the exact seed and accepted evidence identity to survive every downstream engine.

**How to apply:** Any new composition policy must fit into the declared precedence, remain inert for v1 plans, and propagate composition version plus evidence identity into final TrackModel provenance.
