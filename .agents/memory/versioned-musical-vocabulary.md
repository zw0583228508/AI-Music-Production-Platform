---
name: Versioned musical vocabulary
description: Compatibility and evidence rules for style grammar and approved generation preferences.
---

Every declared style or producer-preference dimension must map to a consumed pre-note musical decision; changing only metadata, ranking, or a generic random seed is not a semantic implementation.

**Why:** Aggregate style comparisons can hide inert vocabulary dimensions, while applying new behavior to historical directives silently changes deterministic replay.

**How to apply:** Use one-dimension-at-a-time sensitivity fixtures with fixed unrelated inputs. Gate new behavior on an explicit contract version, preserve old seed formulas and directive semantics, and treat historical approvals missing new evidence as safely ineligible rather than fatal.
