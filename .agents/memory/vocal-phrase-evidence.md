---
name: Vocal phrase evidence
description: Evidence boundaries for vocal phrases, breaths, symbolic alignment, and arrangement space.
---

Phrase and breath evidence must originate from decoded PCM of a verified vocal stem. A breath is only a bounded silent gap between accepted phrases; leading or trailing silence is arrangement space, not a claimed breath.

**Why:** Full-mix activity, lyrics, melody, and section templates can suggest vocal structure but cannot prove that a singer voiced a phrase or took a breath. Treating those suggestions as observations would create convincing unsupported evidence.

**How to apply:** Keep phrase, breath, lyric alignment, melody alignment, and arrangement-space statuses independent. Project accepted timed evidence through the canonical tempo/meter timeline, and return explicit unavailable or conflicting states with empty alignments whenever required evidence is absent or incompatible.
